Remember

Capture first

Enter one item per non-empty line and choose Save on device, or press Ctrl/⌘ + Enter. Saving does not require a list, questions or AI. Notes, list, or context opens everyday choices; the List selector includes projects and areas. Set dates, waiting, time and energy later from the item's editor in Process. Comma/semicolon splitting is a preview: edit the lines before saving to confirm. Original input is retained.

Optional local AI

Review suggested tasks

The robot beside the header save icon shows this device's AI agent: green means ready, yellow means busy, and red means a download is needed or an error occurred. A red crossed circle means unavailable in this browser or on this device. Press the robot to download and prepare the model or retry an error; its tooltip describes the current state.

When Local AI is available, open Local AI suggestions in Capture to enable suggested text at the cursor after a typing pause. Tab or Use suggested text inserts it; Escape dismisses it. Save your capture when ready. Suggest tasks now can download the browser model and shows a task batch to review beside Capture. Include my existing list names shows which names are supplied as context; changing it refreshes automatic suggestions. The choice is saved for this workspace and account on this device.

Review saved suggestions lets you edit, add, remove or merge tasks; only Accept all tasks on device saves them as tasks. Merge into previous task preserves the removed task's notes and attributes in the previous task's notes. Stop and keep review preserves corrections offline. Suggestions never replace an existing review. Manual one-item-per-line capture also remains available.

Check every inferred deadline and attribute. Dates use the capture timezone; date-only deadlines have no added time. Accepted tasks retain the exact source. Oversized batches save nothing; copy or export the draft before reducing it.

Organize

Process and lists

Select a list and choose Add item, or select a project and choose Add next action. The editor starts with that destination selected; project additions start as next actions. After creating a list or project, use its add button to begin immediately. Your Capture draft stays separate. Finish saving an unfinished edit before adding another item.

The hierarchy icon opens Process. Its view selector starts with Inbox (unprocessed); choose another list, project, planned day or All items to process other work. The stacked-layers icon opens a specific list and its defaults. Completed and reference items are hidden initially; choose their status or All statuses to find them. File useful non-actionable information as Reference using Status in the editor or Clarify. It stays editable, outside action queues and new reviews; change its status when it becomes actionable.

Close editor or Escape keeps unfinished text without reopening the editor on your next visit. Use Resume edit to continue, or Discard edit draft to remove only that unfinished edit. Saving an unchanged record closes it without creating a task change. Drafts stay with their account and workspace on this device.

In List Workspace, expand Context, time & energy to narrow the current list. Execute has the same filters for working through a collection. Choose next in Status to focus on next actions. Available time is a maximum; available energy includes that level and lower levels. Tasks with missing or unrecognized time/energy estimates stay visible. Context matches the chosen context; No context finds unclassified tasks. Reset context, time & energy clears these limits while keeping your list or Execute selection. Each workspace saves separate choices for List Workspace and Execute on this device, including offline.

Tap a task, list or project title to edit it in a text box. Save and Close stay below the scrolling fields. The check mark completes a task; the return arrow reopens it. The three-dot menu contains Clarify, Brief and available undo actions. The magnifying-glass icon opens daily and weekly reviews in a page card beside the Capture, Process and List Workspace destinations. Menu is collapsible at every screen size and contains sync, workspace management, Export, defaults, preferences, recovery, sharing and sign-out.

Do

Work through a checklist

The clipboard icon opens Execute. Choose a collection type, then choose a collection and check each ready item as you finish it. Area, Role, Initiative, Program and Reference list buttons appear when that type exists; items linked directly to an Area can be worked without a Project. Inbox items remain available for ordinary lists and checklists; Next items are ready actions. Waiting, Deferred, Someday, Reference, Dropped, Completed and custom statuses stay out of the default work queue. A due waiting or deferred cue means review the item and choose its next state; the app never promotes it automatically. Use Inspect saved work in List Workspace to open the same collection with All statuses and cleared context, time and energy filters. Expand Context, time & energy to narrow ready items; reset those filters to see more. Checklists created before this update may still have Kind set to List; edit their Kind in List Workspace to move them to Checklist. Tap an item title to open its details in the editor. Execute remembers its collection and filters for this account and workspace on this device, including offline. Device saves sync when you reconnect.

Keep your work

Device copies, sync and recovery

Expand the status indicator to see the account, offline readiness and save details. A check mark means saved work is server-confirmed; a clock means pending saves; a circle means offline; a warning means a save needs attention. Color is an extra cue. Errors and recovery actions appear in the workspace.

Sign in online once and wait for “Ready to reopen this inbox offline” before going offline. An offline setup warning means reopening is not ready yet: expand the indicator for instructions. A first visit without internet cannot load the app. Install from Preferences when supported, or use its installation help.

Device copies belong to this browser profile. Unsynced work cannot be recovered after clearing site storage or losing this device. Export a copy before signing out or changing devices. Use a private browser profile on shared devices. Installation does not back up unsynced work.

Export device copy includes all work available on this device, pending saves and drafts. Newer server changes may be missing; sync first when online. Portable JSON preserves the full recovery copy; Readable text works in an ordinary text editor. Downloaded copies remain outside the app after sign-out or deletion.

Export server copy reads saved work across your devices through a fixed cutoff at the start of the export. It requires a connection and excludes pending device saves and drafts. Choose Portable JSON or Readable text; cancel at any time. Failed or cancelled exports do not download a partial file or change your work. Keep a device copy too when you have unsynced work.

Pending saves remain bound to the original account. If a session expires, sign in to that account to resume. Failed saves retain your text and offer comparison/recovery; a stale edit cannot silently replace a newer server version.

Menu → Edit recovery → Undo last edit restores the fields changed by your latest item, list or project editor save in this browser and account. It is available for seven days from that device save, until another editor save or a change to the same record. It survives reload, offline use and sync. Undo queues a new version-checked save; a conflict keeps the server version and offers recovery. Capture and editor drafts stay in place. Creation, deletion, defaults, clarification, reviews and briefs use their existing recovery paths; this control has one step and no redo.

The seven-day limit is an undo window, not a data-purge promise. Recovery text can remain in browser storage and downloaded device copies; server history and backups follow their existing retention. Undo recovery is local to this browser profile, while confirmed edits and undo saves sync to your other devices.

When an update is ready, wait for your draft to be saved on device, close all app tabs/windows and reopen. Pending saves stay on this device.

Reflect

Daily and weekly reviews

Daily reviews cover next actions, today's plan and due cues. Weekly reviews cover active items and projects, including inbox, waiting and deferred work. Reviews use work available on this device; sync first for newer changes.

Edit or Clarify opens the existing task tools and returns to your review position. Projects show their unfinished actions and offer Add next action. After inspecting changes, choose a decision: Complete item or Make next updates status, preserves dates and supports Undo selected decision. Editing alone does not mark a record reviewed.

Retain keeps the current state without adding a date. Drop keeps the text but marks the item dropped. Defer sets a review start date, not a deadline. Use Stop and close review or another navigation icon to leave the review card; your position stays saved. Resume it on this page at any time, including offline. Undo is available until the reviewed record changes again; there is no timer.

Larger inventories use batches of up to 200 records. Finish a batch, then choose Review next batch; retained commitments do not need to be completed or dropped. Previous batches remain in Saved reviews. Decision history is kept separately, so undo and choosing again do not fill the review.

Decide

Clarification and optional guidance

Choose what the capture means directly. Make project, area, role or list converts it into that container in one save. Parent mode lets you select where the new container belongs before choosing Make. A converted source remains in recoverable history and Undo conversion is available while the new container is unchanged and empty.

File item adds a visible list or project membership without classifying or completing the capture. Parent selects a container relationship but does not save by itself. Search filters the same destination list; duplicate names include their parent path.

Action shows editable wording and Next, Waiting, Plan, Defer and Done choices beside the destinations. A destination tap saves the disposition and relationship together; Save without a new destination preserves existing memberships. Reference and Someday use the same destination surface. Move to Deleted remains recoverable under More.

Clarify inbox works through the captures that were present when the session began. Skip leaves an item in Inbox; Stop saves the current wording and position. Each successful device save advances immediately, including offline. Undo previous decision remains until another decision is committed. Draft projects are linkable, appear as Needs outcome and enter weekly review; add a real desired outcome before activating one.

Optional English AI suggestions run on this device when supported. The browser may download a model. Review suggestions before using them; they are not accepted facts. Manual clarification remains available. Saved work still syncs to your cloud account.

Prepare

Brief revisions

Typing keeps a device draft. Saved revisions and decisions sync with your account. Review the template and unknowns; saving edited content creates a new draft revision. Acceptance applies only to the selected revision. Nothing is sent automatically.

Export labels drafts and pending decisions as unaccepted or unconfirmed; only a server-confirmed accepted revision is labeled accepted.

Lists, projects and areas

Use New list to create a List, Project, Area, Role, Initiative, Program or Reference list. Active, Someday and completed projects need a desired outcome; Clarify may create a linkable Needs outcome draft. Choose an optional parent to nest collections; Include nested items shows each task only once.

Organize in lets one task belong to several collections without copying it. Ordinary capture stays in Inbox until clarified. Primary memberships control list defaults and what older app versions can show.

Changing a list or project's workspace carries its nested collections and linked items while preserving their IDs and history. A tree of up to 20 affected records moves atomically. Larger trees are saved as a resumable device move: the app temporarily detaches relationships, moves the original records in batches, then restores relationships inside the moved tree. Links to collections left behind are cleared. Reloading, going offline or losing an acknowledgement keeps completed batches and resumes on reconnect. If a concurrent edit, deletion or archived workspace pauses the move, inspect the saved conflict, restore or unarchive as needed, then choose Resume collection move. Another device may temporarily show detached records during a large move, but never a relationship across workspaces.

Reference lists show reusable entries. Use as checklist copies 1–19 selected entries into a named ordinary list, leaving the source unchanged. Copies enter Inbox unless you explicitly choose Next actions.

Create/link an area from a tag preserves your tags and links only this workspace's items. Save another batch to continue large mappings; already linked items are skipped. Empty a collection of items and children before deleting it.
